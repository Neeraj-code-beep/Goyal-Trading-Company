## Content Structure Analysis

### Information Architecture Problems
**Current Issues:**
- Flat structure with no clear hierarchy
- Product categories not logically grouped
- No content categorization
- Poor user journey mapping
- No content relationships

**Recommended Structure:**
1. **Homepage** - Overview, value proposition, key CTAs
2. **About** - Company story, team, history
3. **Manufacturing** - Capabilities, facility, quality
4. **Products** - Organized catalog with filtering
5. **Industries** - Served markets and use cases
5. **Clients** - Testimonials, case studies
6. **Resources** - Catalogs, guides, insights
7. **Contact** - Multiple contact methods, forms

### Content Hierarchy Issues
**Current Problems:**
- No clear content prioritization
- All content given equal weight
- No visual hierarchy
- Poor scannability
- Long text blocks

**Recommended Hierarchy:**
1. **Primary Content** (Above fold, high priority)
   - Value proposition
   - Key products
   - Trust signals
   - Primary CTAs

2. **Secondary Content** (Below fold, medium priority)
   - Detailed product information
   - Manufacturing capabilities
   - Client testimonials
   - Company story

3. **Tertiary Content** (Deep pages, lower priority)
   - Detailed specifications
   - Technical documentation
   - Historical information
   - Policy details
