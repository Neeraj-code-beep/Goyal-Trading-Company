# Website Audit - Goyal Trading Company

## Current Website URL
https://www.goyaltradingcompany.com/

## Audit Date
July 9, 2026

## Overall Assessment
The existing website serves as a business information source but lacks modern design, conversion optimization, and premium positioning expected from a B2B wholesale manufacturer.

## Navigation Analysis

### Main Navigation Structure
**Observed Menu Items:**
- Home
- Company Profile
- Products (with dropdown categories)
- Contact Us
- Site Map

**Navigation Issues:**
- No clear hierarchy in product categories
- Dropdown menus appear cluttered
- No mega menu for extensive product catalog
- No search functionality visible
- No breadcrumb navigation
- Inconsistent navigation across pages

### Product Navigation
**Category Structure:**
- School Wear
- Corporate Wear
- Promotion Wear
- Medical Equipments
- Gym Bags
- Men Activewear
- Jackets
- Swimming
- Men Shorts

**Navigation Problems:**
- Too many product categories without clear grouping
- No filtering options
- No sorting capabilities
- No product search
- Deep navigation structure (3-4 levels deep)
- No clear categorization logic
