## Key Differentiators Identified

### What Sets Premium Manufacturers Apart

**1. Specificity Over Generality**
- Exact equipment lists vs. "modern machinery"
- Specific capacity numbers vs. "large capacity"
- Detailed MOQ policies vs. "flexible MOQ"
- Precise lead times vs. "fast delivery"

**2. Transparency Over Secrecy**
- Open pricing ranges vs. "contact for pricing"
- Public certifications vs. "certified upon request"
- Visible processes vs. "quality assured"
- Real factory photos vs. stock imagery

**3. Education Over Promotion**
- Cost factor explanations vs. "best prices"
- Fabric guides vs. "quality fabrics"
- Process documentation vs. "expert manufacturing"
- Industry insights vs. "industry leader"

**4. Access Over Barriers**
- Direct contact options vs. complex forms
- Named contacts vs. generic info
- Multiple channels vs. single method
- Self-service options vs. manual processes

**Recommended Pattern:**
