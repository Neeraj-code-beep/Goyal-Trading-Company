# Lead Generation Strategy - Goyal Trading Company

## Lead Generation Overview

**Objective:** Transform website visitors into qualified B2B leads through strategic conversion optimization and trust-building mechanisms.

**Target:** 3-5x increase in qualified inquiries within 6 months of website launch.

## Lead Generation Channels

### 1. Website-Based Lead Generation

#### Primary Conversion Paths

**Request for Quote (RFQ)**
- **Purpose:** Capture detailed manufacturing inquiries
- **Placement:** Homepage, product pages, dedicated RFQ page, navigation
- **Form Fields:**
  - Name and Company
  - Email and Phone
  - Product Category
  - Quantity/MOQ Requirements
  - Specifications (fabric, sizes, colors)
  - Tech Pack Upload (PDF, AI, CAD)
  - Timeline Requirements
  - Additional Notes
- **Conversion Goal:** 40-60% improvement over current process

**Sample Request**
- **Purpose:** Enable product sampling before bulk orders
- **Placement:** Product pages, dedicated sample request page
- **Form Fields:**
  - Contact Information
  - Product Selection
  - Sample Quantity
  - Customization Requirements
  - Shipping Address
  - Payment Method
- **Conversion Goal:** Capture 20-30% of product page visitors

**General Inquiry**
- **Purpose:** Capture general questions and partnership discussions
- **Placement:** Contact page, throughout site
- **Form Fields:**
  - Name and Company
  - Email and Phone
  - Inquiry Type (General, Partnership, Distribution)
  - Message
  - Preferred Contact Method
- **Conversion Goal:** Capture 15-25% of contact page visitors

#### Secondary Conversion Paths

**WhatsApp Integration**
- **Purpose:** Instant communication for mobile users
- **Placement:** Floating button, contact page, product pages
- **Functionality:** Direct WhatsApp chat with sales team
- **Target:** 30-40% of mobile inquiries

**Phone Calls**
- **Purpose:** Immediate response for urgent inquiries
- **Placement:** Header, footer, contact page
- **Functionality:** Click-to-call on mobile
- **Target:** 20-30% of urgent inquiries

**Email Capture**
- **Purpose:** Lead nurturing and catalog distribution
- **Placement:** Homepage, resource downloads
- **Functionality:** Email capture for downloadable resources
- **Target:** 15-20% of resource page visitors

## Lead Qualification Framework

### Lead Scoring System

#### Demographic Scoring (40 points)
- **Company Type:**
  - International Brand: 15 points
  - Domestic Retailer: 10 points
  - Institution: 12 points
  - Government: 15 points
  - Individual: 5 points
- **Company Size:**
  - Large (500+ employees): 10 points
  - Medium (50-500 employees): 7 points
  - Small (10-50 employees): 5 points
  - Micro (<10 employees): 3 points
- **Geographic Location:**
  - International (US, UK, Europe): 10 points
  - International (Other): 7 points
  - Metro India: 5 points
  - Other India: 3 points

#### Behavioral Scoring (35 points)
- **Inquiry Type:**
  - RFQ with specifications: 15 points
  - Sample request: 12 points
  - General inquiry: 8 points
- **Quantity:**
  - Bulk (1000+ units): 10 points
  - Medium (100-999 units): 7 points
  - Small (10-99 units): 4 points
  - Sample (<10 units): 2 points
- **Engagement:**
  - Multiple website visits: 5 points
  - Resource downloads: 3 points
  - Single visit: 1 point

#### Firmographic Scoring (25 points)
- **Budget Indicators:**
  - Specific budget mentioned: 10 points
  - Price range inquiry: 7 points
  - No budget mentioned: 3 points
- **Timeline:**
  - Immediate need: 8 points
  - 1-3 months: 5 points
  - 3-6 months: 3 points
  - Exploratory: 1 point
- **Decision Authority:**
  - Decision maker: 7 points
  - Influencer: 4 points
  - Researcher: 2 points

### Lead Classification

**Hot Leads (70+ points):**
- Immediate follow-up within 2 hours
- Direct sales team assignment
- Priority response
- Sample offer if applicable

**Warm Leads (50-69 points):**
- Follow-up within 24 hours
- Sales team assignment
- Standard response process
- Nurture campaign

**Cold Leads (30-49 points):**
- Follow-up within 48 hours
- Automated nurturing
- Email campaign
- Periodic check-ins

**Unqualified (<30 points):**
- Automated response
- Add to general newsletter
- Monitor for re-engagement

## Lead Nurturing Strategy

### Email Nurturing Sequences

#### New Lead Sequence (5 emails over 2 weeks)
1. **Immediate:** Thank you and next steps
2. **Day 3:** Company capabilities overview
3. **Day 7:** Relevant case study
4. **Day 10:** Product catalog
5. **Day 14:** Check-in and offer assistance

#### Sample Request Sequence (4 emails over 3 weeks)
1. **Immediate:** Sample request confirmation
2. **Day 7:** Sample production update
3. **Day 14:** Shipping notification
4. **Day 21:** Feedback request and next steps

#### RFQ Sequence (6 emails over 4 weeks)
1. **Immediate:** RFQ acknowledgment
2. **Day 3:** Additional information request (if needed)
3. **Day 10:** Quote delivery
4. **Day 14:** Follow-up on quote
5. **Day 21:** Additional information or offer
6. **Day 28:** Final check-in

## Conversion Rate Optimization

### A/B Testing Priorities

#### Homepage Testing
- Hero headline variations
- CTA button text and color
- Trust signal placement
- Form vs. direct contact options

#### Product Page Testing
- Image layouts
- CTA placement
- Information density
- Related product recommendations

#### Form Testing
- Field length and complexity
- CTA button design
- Trust signals near forms
- Multi-step vs. single-step forms

### Conversion Barriers to Address

**Current Barriers:**
- OTP verification complexity
- Non-functional JavaScript CTAs
- No clear value proposition
- Weak trust signals
- Poor mobile experience

**Solutions:**
- Remove OTP for international buyers
- Implement functional, reliable CTAs
- Clear value proposition above fold
- Prominent trust signals
- Mobile-first responsive design
